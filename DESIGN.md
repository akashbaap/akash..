## Brand & Style
This design system embodies academic authority, high-performance competitive achievement, and reassuring accessibility for parents and students in Kanpur and Northern India. Drawing inspiration from top-tier coaching institutions and premium educational platforms, the personality is grounded in proven success, intellectual rigor, and forward-looking momentum. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with High-Contrast Editorial Prestige**. It pairs deep regal midnight blues with brilliant amber-gold accents and crisp, luminous white containers. The design avoids clutter while embracing bold visual hierarchy, strategic social proof (rankers, board scores, faculty credentials), and conversion-engineered contact mechanisms (quick call, WhatsApp enquiry, scholarship trial registration). The emotional response should be confidence, trust, aspiration, and immediate motivation to enroll.

## Colors
The color foundation is built on deep royal navy blue (`#12174D`) as the primary shade, representing structural stability, discipline, and institutional legacy. 

- **Primary (`#12174D`)**: Anchors global headers, high-prestige hero segments, card outlines, and primary typography.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Radiant academic gold evoking merit, medals, honor rolls, badges, and primary action triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#F37021`)**: Energetic flame amber-orange utilized for high-urgency notifications, admission countdowns, special batch alerts, and interactive conversion accents.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Rich slate black for maximum legibility in body typography, balanced against soft warm-slate background neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) and stark white surface cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Supportive Accents**: Crisp verification green (`#16A34A` / `#25D366`) dedicated to WhatsApp integration and verified topper testaments.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy leverages **Outfit** for headlines and display elements, introducing geometric precision, high-impact readability, and athletic educational authority. Uppercase treatment is selectively employed for badge tags, course certifications (ICSE | ISC | CBSE | NCERT), and honor roll categories.

For running prose, data displays, and interactive form fields, **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides humanist warmth, exceptional clarity on mobile devices, and balanced vertical metrics. Numbers and percentiles (e.g., "98.67%", "Rank-9 in UP") feature semi-bold to extra-bold tabular figures for immediate impact.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column grid system bounded at a maximum desktop width of 1280px to preserve comfortable scanning speed and density. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns with `gutter` at 1.5rem (24px) and page edge margins of 2.5rem (40px). Allows multi-column student honor roll showcases (4 to 5 cards per row) and sticky admission lead forms beside curriculum details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with 1.25rem gutters and 2rem side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `gutter-mobile` at 1rem (16px) and `margin-mobile` at 1.25rem (20px). Critical elements (call buttons, batch schedules, result ribbons) shift to vertical stack or horizontal swipe strips.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system uses balanced structural layering rather than excessive drop shadows, reinforcing trust and clarity:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) or light slate tint (`#F8FAFC`) with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Background `#FFFFFF` with a crisp border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and subtle, ambient elevation: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 23, 77, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(18, 23, 77, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Floats)**: Used when hovering topper profiles, course options, and contact modals: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(18, 23, 77, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(18, 23, 77, 0.08)` accompanied by a subtle 2px upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Drawers)**: High-priority bottom floating bar for direct calling and WhatsApp enquiries: `0 -4px 20px rgba(18, 23, 77, 0.15)`.
- **Prestige Glow**: Accent gold containers utilize an interior radial gradient or soft warm halo: `0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of `2` provides a polished, approachable, yet firmly structured geometry.
- Standard interactive elements (input fields, buttons, standard tiles) feature `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px).
- Surface cards, testimonial dialogs, and hero banners feature `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- Badges, status chips, and topper percentage laurels use full circular or pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo gold seal medallions and medal iconography.
- Director / Faculty and Topper portrait crops use circular frames (`rounded-full`) framed with dual-ring gold borders (`ring-4 ring-[#F59E0B] ring-offset-2`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions / Enrolment)**: Solid energetic gold/amber background (`#F59E0B`), bold navy text (`#12174D`), uppercase label with medium letter tracking, rounded radius (0.5rem), hover state transition to `#D97706`.
- **Secondary (Contact / WhatsApp)**: Deep royal navy background (`#12174D`), white text, crisp amber border (`1.5px solid #F59E0B`), hover background `#1A1E5C`.
- **Direct Calling / Emergency Action**: Emerald green variant (`#16A34A` / `#25D366`) featuring embedded phone/WhatsApp glyphs with high contrast white text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curriculum Chips**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`), soft navy background (`#EEF2FF`), saturated navy text (`#12174D`), and delicate borders (`1px solid #CBD5E1`).
- **Honor & Percentile Laurels**: Amber gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B, #EA580C)`), bright white text, bold tracking, often paired with laurel or star icons.

### Input Fields & Enquiry Form
- **Form Controls**: Crisp white background, `1.5px` border in `#CBD5E1`. Focused state transitions instantly to deep navy border (`#12174D`) with a soft gold focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`).
- **Labels**: High-contrast, top-aligned bold label font with optional subtle helper text.

### Cards & Displays
- **Topper / Star Card**: Vertical orientation with circular avatar, prominent gold laurel badge displaying percentage/rank, student name in `headline-sm`, and course batch beneath. Surface is pure white against slate, enclosed in a fine neutral border that transforms to gold on hover.
- **Faculty Spotlight Card**: Two-column layout pairing a rounded portrait with credentials, subjects mastered (e.g., *M.Sc., B.Ed., M.Ed., IIT-JAM*), and direct batch links.
- **Course Program Card**: Navy-bordered container with curriculum badge headers (ICSE / CBSE / Foundation), key features in checkmarked lists, and an inline fee enquiry button.

### Lists & Checkboxes
- **Academic Feature Lists**: Bullet points replaced with custom circular tick icons in amber/gold (`#F59E0B`) on navy backgrounds.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Deep navy filled states with white checks, using a smooth 0.2s transition.
